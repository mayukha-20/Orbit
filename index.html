What the charts, badges, and scores mean · Why we're different from a normal watchlist

Most watchlists show the same thing: a price and a % change from yesterday. The problem is — a 2% move means very different things depending on the stock. A 2% drop on stable Infosys is big news. A 2% drop on a volatile small-cap is a quiet Tuesday. Orbit scores each move relative to that stock's own normal behavior, so you only get alerted when something truly unusual happens for that specific stock.

Step 1 — Learn normal Each stock gets a personal volatility baseline We look at the last 20 trading days and compute the average daily price swing — weighted so recent days count more. This is the stock's "normal". A stable stock might normally move 0.5%/day. A volatile one might move 3%/day.
Step 2 — Measure the move Compare today vs what you last saw When you return to Orbit, we compare the current price to what it was the last time you opened the app. We then express that move as a multiple of the stock's normal swing — e.g. "2.1× its usual daily range."
Step 3 — Surface what's unusual Only alert on moves that matter If the move is ≥ 1.5× normal, or if trading volume is ≥ 1.5× normal, it's flagged as meaningful and rises to the top. Calm stocks collapse below. You see the most important thing first.
What each element on a stock card means
Cyan chart — 20 trading days of market prices (from Yahoo Finance)
Green chart — Your own check-in history (price each time you visited)
Volatility bar — How erratic this stock normally is (wider = more volatile)
Range gauge — Where today's price sits between its 52-week low and high
Change score bar — How unusual the move is. The marker at 1.5× is the "meaningful" threshold
LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH VOL badge — This stock's personality: how much it typically moves per day
VOL ↑ / VOL ↓ badge — Whether this stock is becoming MORE or LESS volatile recently

⏱ Data delay: Yahoo Finance (yfinance) provides ~15–20 minute delayed quotes on the free tier. The price shown is not a real-time tick — it is the most recent available quote. Historical daily closing prices used for EWMA are fully accurate.
